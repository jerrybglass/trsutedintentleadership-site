Privacy

Last updated August 9, 2026

Information collected through this website

Trusted Intent may receive information you choose to provide through the speaking inquiry form or email-list signup, including your name, organization, contact information, event details, and email address. The site may also collect standard technical information about visits through analytics and hosting services.

How the information is used

Information submitted through the speaking form is used to respond to your inquiry and discuss event fit, availability, and potential services. Email addresses submitted through the book-update form are used to send the updates and announcements requested by the subscriber. Website analytics help Glass Leadership Group understand how the site is used and improve its content and performance.

Service providers

The website uses Formspree to process speaking inquiries, Mailchimp to manage email subscriptions, Google Analytics to understand website traffic, and GitHub Pages to host the site. Information submitted or collected through those services is also subject to their respective privacy practices.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from email updates using the link included in each message. To ask about information you submitted through this website, request a correction, or request deletion, email blake@trustedintentleadership.com.
